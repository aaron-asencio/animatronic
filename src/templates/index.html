Awake mode

Scan mode

The head uses the camera to scan for people (and dogs). When someone is detected the head keeps tracking them while an arm-only gesture or routine runs — gestures fire ~5x more often than routines. Runs until the timeout elapses or you stop it; pressing any routine or movement button also winds it down so your action runs. Start/stop is in the top controls.

Puppeteer mode

A live-performance mode: you voice the figure through the mic while the head tracks a detected person and you trigger arm gestures by hand. The head keeps only the neck (so arm-only gestures still work over the top), detection never auto-fires a routine, and if the camera loses the person the head recenters and waits rather than stopping. Runs until you stop it (or another mode/routine preempts it). Start/stop is in the top controls.
